# Data licence and attribution

`data/talis2024_kaz_analysis_subset.csv` is a subset of the OECD TALIS 2024 public-use teacher file (`btgintt4.csv`). The subset keeps the Kazakhstan records and 34 of the 611 variables, with all values unchanged.

The data remain subject to the **OECD Terms and Conditions** (<https://www.oecd.org/termsandconditions>). Under those terms, users may "extract from, download, copy, adapt, print, distribute, share and embed Data for any purpose", provided that appropriate credit is given to the OECD. The same acknowledgment requirement must be included in any sub-licence.

**Required attribution:**

> OECD (2025), *TALIS 2024 Database*, OECD Publishing, Paris, https://www.oecd.org/en/data/datasets/talis-2024-database.html.

Anyone redistributing this subset must keep this attribution requirement. The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the code only, not the data.
